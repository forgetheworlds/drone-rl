where GT is occupied: the dangerous error). Runtimes: single-thread on the box, scaled to Pi Zero 2 W
and ESP32-P4 cores with rough factors (10x, 30x); not measured on target.

## Methods

- `bm160_b12_r5`: bm160_lrc_b12, estimates beyond 5 m dropped
- `bm160_lrc`: StereoBM 160x120, 32 disp, block 9, texture 10, uniq 15, L/R check
- `bm160_lrc_b12`: StereoBM 160x120 at 12 cm baseline (48 disp)
- `bm160_lrc_epf`: bm160_lrc + edge-anchored plane fill (per frame)
- `bm160_lrc_epf30`: bm160_lrc_epf with anchors on only 30% of the hole border (still >= 3 sides)
- `bm160_temporal`: bm160_lrc + 3-frame VO-warped vote / history fill
- `bm160_temporal_map`: bm160_temporal + holes filled by raycasting the fused log-odds map (VO poses)
- `bm160_temporal_map_epf`: bm160_temporal + map raycast hole fill, then edge-anchored plane fill
- `bm160_temporal_r3`: bm160_temporal, estimates beyond 3 m dropped
- `bm160_temporal_r3_epf`: bm160_temporal_r3 + edge-anchored plane fill after the 3-frame vote
- `bm160_temporal_r3_epf30`: bm160_temporal_r3_epf with anchors on only 30% of the hole border (still >= 3 sides)
- `sgbm320`: baseline: OpenCV SGBM 3-way, 64 disp, block 5, uniq 10, speckle 50
- `sgbm320_epf`: sgbm320 baseline + edge-anchored plane fill
- `sgbm320_lrc_tex`: SGBM + CLAHE + L/R check (1 px) + uniq 15 + Sobel texture gate + d>=2
- `sgbm320_lrc_tex_epf`: sgbm320_lrc_tex + edge-anchored plane fill
- `sgbm320_planefill`: filtered SGBM + RANSAC plane fill of textureless holes from their valid border
- `sgbm320_temporal_r3`: sgbm320_temporal, estimates beyond 3 m dropped
