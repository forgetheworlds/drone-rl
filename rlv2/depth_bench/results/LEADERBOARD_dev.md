## Methods

- `bm160_b12_r5`: bm160_lrc_b12, estimates beyond 5 m dropped
- `bm160_b12_temporal`: bm160_lrc_b12 + 3-frame VO-warped vote / history fill
- `bm160_lrc`: StereoBM 160x120, 32 disp, block 9, texture 10, uniq 15, L/R check
- `bm160_lrc_b12`: StereoBM 160x120 at 12 cm baseline (48 disp)
- `bm160_temporal`: bm160_lrc + 3-frame VO-warped vote / history fill
- `bm160_temporal_map`: bm160_temporal + holes filled by raycasting the fused log-odds map (VO poses)
- `bm160_temporal_r3`: bm160_temporal, estimates beyond 3 m dropped
- `bm320_lrc`: StereoBM 320x240, 64 disp, block 11, L/R check
- `planesweep_mv160`: multi-view plane sweep (stereo pair + 2 past frames, VO poses), 64 inv-depth planes
- `sgbm160_lrc_tex`: same at 160x120 (4x cheaper)
- `sgbm320`: baseline: OpenCV SGBM 3-way, 64 disp, block 5, uniq 10, speckle 50
- `sgbm320_lrc_tex`: SGBM + CLAHE + L/R check (1 px) + uniq 15 + Sobel texture gate + d>=2
- `sgbm320_lrc_tex_b12`: 12 cm baseline, 96 disp
- `sgbm320_lrc_tex_map`: sgbm320_lrc_tex + holes filled by raycasting the fused log-odds map (VO poses)
- `sgbm320_planefill`: filtered SGBM + RANSAC plane fill of textureless holes from their valid border
- `sgbm320_temporal`: filtered SGBM + 3-frame VO-warped vote (keep if >=1 past agrees) + history fill (2 agree)
- `sgbm320_temporal_planefill`: temporal vote/fill, then RANSAC plane fill
- `sgbm320_temporal_r3`: sgbm320_temporal, estimates beyond 3 m dropped
- `sgbm320_wls_conf`: SGBM + WLS filter (lambda 8000, sigma 1.5), keep WLS confidence > 0.6
- `sgbm320_wls_dense`: WLS filled output, no confidence gate (fills textureless walls by propagation)
